A dense one-page newspaper, "THE WORLD TODAY", summing up the world
news of **Tuesday 24 November 2026** in a single 1024x1024 image:
a big date band, an all-text lead story, six story cards, a ten-line
IN BRIEF block and an ALSO TODAY ticker - about 29 of the day's
stories on one page.

The day's stories on the page:

1. **Lead - Markets / Nvidia:** Nvidia reinstates co-founder Jensen
   Huang as CEO, 12 days after he quit over an affair admission;
   Trump called into the board meeting; the stock leaps 31% on
   Monday (Nasdaq +6%, S&P 500 +5%, chip index +12%).
2. **Europe at war:** Russian strikes kill 15 in the Kyiv region -
   a Dymer apartment block collapses (10 dead, a boy of 7 freed
   after 14 hours); Zelenskyy counts 2,700 drones and 165 missiles
   in a week and presses the US to release paid-for Patriot missiles.
3. **Middle East:** Trump tells Fox he is inclined to strike Iran
   after the new year if there is no deal; Brent falls below $90
   (-4.2% to $88.67) as Libya's Sharara pipeline restarts; Houthis
   claim a hit on Saudi Arabia's Rabigh refinery.
4. **Business:** CrowdStrike buys Zscaler in an all-stock deal
   valued at $43.9 billion.
5. **Climate:** the COP31 fallout rolls on - the Antalya talks ended
   with no deal and a final draft silent on fossil fuels; leftovers
   go to COP32 in Ethiopia in 2027.
6. **Asia - Disaster:** 24 dead in the Cikande candy factory fire in
   Serang, Banten, Indonesia; rescuers search for dozens missing.
7. **Sport - NFL:** the 49ers rout the Vikings 35-10 at Mexico
   City's Azteca (Purdy, 3 TDs); Bills 26-9 Dolphins; Lions 31-21
   Buccaneers; Cowboys 51-30 Titans.

Plus a ten-line IN BRIEF block (US-Syria demining training talks,
FBI deputy director Raia retiring, Democrats' biggest House majority
since the 1990s, euro zone and US PMIs, the Brussels Airport strike,
Tokyo's reopening after the Nikkei's 5.06% Friday fall, Kospi and
Taiex moves, DR Congo Ebola passing 11,000 infections, the Bayelsa
funeral boat disaster) and an ALSO TODAY ticker (Trump's meme-coin
gala, Miley Cyrus's wedding, Shuri Castle's reopening, Taiwan's
count of Chinese aircraft, Iran's 76.8% inflation).

The image is drawn by `draw_image.py` (Pillow, deterministic) and
committed as `entry.png`; `main.py` copies it to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
